The title was inspired by a [tweet](https://x.com/russdiemon/status/2122508023952548144?a=T31qznBy-bBA30GQuHU&s=20) that sums up how I feel in this moment, and I'd imagine how a lot of people feel too. (To those who feel the same: if I could wrap my arms around you physically, I would, but please accept a digital hug from me as a pittance.) Yes, it's been a while since I last wrote, and I seem to say that every time I sit down to write. But it's been both a momentous couple of weeks and a calamitous couple of weeks. As someone once put it best, it's been the best of times and the worst of times.

I reached a breaking point yesterday, one that made me question who I am and what I want to do and be. It's not a midlife crisis (I've already had mine), but more an affirmation of what I have to do. In some ways, I'm saying this from rock bottom: I'm somewhat defeated, but I'm also at the beginning of a journey that I'm truly excited about.

As I've been writing this, I've realised it might give the impression that certain things have happened. They haven't. What has happened is far more complex and nuanced, so much so that even now I struggle to articulate it, even as vaguely as this. Just know there's more I have to plan, research and do in the weeks ahead. But look forward to more blog posts and some more interesting stuff I'm working on. By this time next year, things will have changed; that's guaranteed at this point. I'm not the person I was yesterday, that's for sure. But who I'll be come next year, we'll see.
