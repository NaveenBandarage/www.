As of late, the pen and journaling have become somewhat of a minor stressor for me.

I have a lot on my mind that I know will feel good to get down on paper and out of text messages with friends and loved ones. Yet for some inexplicable reason, I can’t find the courage to open my notebook, pick up my pen and sit down to write. It doesn’t help that I’ve curated a space that’s meant to minimise distractions and make it as easy as possible to write. It doesn’t help that I have the notebook and pen right in front of me, staring at me the way the Green Goblin mask stared at Norman Osborn in _Spider-Man_ (love Willem Dafoe in this film).

Earlier this year, I was on a roll getting my thoughts down in my journal (finally finished my first journal from start to finish). But as of late, while I’ve been able to enter a deeper flow state in various hobbies and interests and even at work, I just, for whatever reason, can’t seem to open my notebook and write. Everything suddenly becomes more interesting, from cleaning to deciding I need to buy something for the home.

Anything to keep the thoughts in my mind from being etched onto the paper in front of me. What’s even more befuddling to me is that I’ve been investing in being able to journal better. I picked up a [Pilot Lightive pen](https://www.pilot.co.jp/press_release/2025/09/29/lightive.html) and a [Midori notebook](https://www.midori-store.net/SHOP/8733/34372/list.html). All the nice frills in the world can’t compel me to get shit down.

I think, though, being able to blog and get this down digitally is the first step into making this a reality. It’s a crazy time. Lots going on, lots that will happen, but I’m trying, I’m training, and I’m getting better. And yes, maybe by the time this blog gets published, I’ll have decided to pick up the pen.

Naveen
